# Irish Killion / Killian leads

Five specific leads appear on this family's research plate. **All five trace to
one source**: Kevin Killion's "Killion/Killian Genealogy" page, where they are
contributor-supplied and unsourced. **[C]**

That does not make them wrong. It makes them **hypotheses with addresses** —
each one names a place and a date specific enough to be checked against a
record, which is the most useful kind of lead there is.

The research behind this file also **corrected three of the five** as the plate
transmits them. Those corrections are below, because searching the wrong spelling
or the wrong date finds nothing and proves nothing.

---

## 1. John Killion of Barnacullen, Rahara parish, Co. Roscommon

> ⚠ **The townland is "Barnacullen", not "Barracullen".** The plate has
> Barracullen; the source page has Barnacullen. An *n* read as an *r*.

- Married **Mary Naughten** of Kiltoom, about **1845**
- Children: **Michael** (b. 1857, Chicago), **Thomas**, **Delia**, **Mary**, and
  possibly **John** and **Catherine**

**Corroboration:** Griffith's Valuation records **3 Killion households in Rahara
parish**, and 2 in Kiltoom — exactly consistent with a Killion of Rahara
marrying a Naughten of Kiltoom. See [surname.md](surname.md).

**To check:** Rahara Catholic parish registers (NLI); Griffith's Valuation for
Barnacullen townland; the Tithe Applotment Books; and, for Michael's 1857 Chicago
birth, Cook County records.

## 3. Thomas Killin of Mullingar — the best-documented of the five

This one already has a named record series at a named archive, which puts it
ahead of the other four.

- Born about **1787** at **Mullingar**
- Enlisted in the **South Cork Militia**, **21 February 1809**
- Volunteered for the **2nd Battalion, 43rd Foot**, **10 May 1809**
- Served in the **Peninsular War**; **pensioned 1824**
- Married **Elizabeth Noon** of Roscommon
- Lived at **Congleton**, then **Coventry**, as a **silk weaver**

> ⚠ **Royal Hospital Chelsea records give him as "Thomas Killen"** — a third
> spelling. Search Killin, Killen and Killian together.

**Where:** Royal Hospital Chelsea pension records, **The National Archives,
Kew** (WO 97 soldiers' documents; WO 116/117 pension admission books). **[A, via C]**

A Peninsular War veteran turned silk weaver is a documentable life. If any of the
five leads can be moved from **[C]** to **[A]** in one archive visit, it is this
one.

## 4 & 5. Roscommon workhouse deaths

From a now-defunct Roscommon Family History Society list. Two corrections to how
the plate transmits them:

| Plate | Source list | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| "Henry **Kilian**, 10 Oct 1887, age 72" | `1887-10-10 **Killian** Henry 72` | **Killian**, not Kilian |
| "John Killion, **6 Jan 1894**, age 21" | `1894-06-01 Killion John 21` | In the list's **year-month-day** format this is **1 June 1894**, not 6 January |

The 1894 date is genuinely ambiguous in the list as published and must be settled
from the original register. But year-month-day is how the rest of the list is
formatted, which favours 1 June.

**Both deaths fall within civil registration (from 1864)**, so both should have a
civil death registration. That makes them the easiest items on this page to
resolve.

**To check, in order:**

1. **irishgenealogy.ie** civil death records for 1887 and 1894
2. **Roscommon Poor Law Union** records — workhouse registers, minute books

A workhouse death at 72 and another at 21 in the same county seven years apart:
if either registration names a parent or a townland, it connects to the Rahara
cluster above.

## The record sets

For any Irish Killion work, in rough order of usefulness:

| Record set | Covers |
|---|---|
| **Civil registration** | deaths and marriages from 1864, births from 1864 — **irishgenealogy.ie** |
| **Griffith's Valuation** | 1847–64 occupiers, by townland |
| **Tithe Applotment Books** | 1823–37 occupiers of agricultural land |
| **Catholic parish registers** | NLI, free online; baptisms and marriages |
| **Poor Law Union records** | workhouse admissions, deaths, minute books |
| **Transportation records** | National Archives of Ireland |

Two transportation entries bear the name **[A]**:

- **Thomas Killian** of Moore parish, death-sentence petition **1820**, ref. PPC 1462
- **James Killion** of Westmeath, **1840**, ref. TR 3 p. 182

Moore parish is in Co. Roscommon, adjacent to the Rahara cluster.

## The standing rule

**Check every lead on this page against irishgenealogy.ie, Griffith's and the
Roscommon Poor Law Union records before entering it in the archive as fact.**

They are good leads. None of them is yet a record.
