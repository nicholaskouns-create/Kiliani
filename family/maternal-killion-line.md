# The maternal line — Kilian / Killian / Killion

> **Generation 1 is not a generation.** Saint Kilian heads this list as the
> *devotional and onomastic root* of the name, which is what the sources support.
> He is not a lineal ancestor and no link from him to the Augsburg family exists
> in any source. See [README.md](README.md).

## Reading the gradient

The pattern is worth naming because it is the honest shape of almost every long
family line, and seeing it clearly is more use than wishing it away.

- **Generations 4–5 are the strongest in the whole line.** Wolfgang and Philipp
  Kilian are in the British Museum, the Deutsche Biographie and the Augsburg
  Stadtlexikon. They are not family tradition; they are catalogued.
- **Generations 6–7 are the break.** Johannes Jacob Balthasar and Johann/Andreas
  are where the Augsburg record and the American record fail to meet. Everything
  downstream of a broken link is provisional no matter how well attested it is in
  itself.
- **Generations 8–11 are derivative but coherent.** Appalachian, plausible,
  record-supported at one remove, not yet inspected at the county level.
- **Generation 12 is firm again.** Obituary and funeral-home records.

So the line has two solid ends and a gap in the middle, and the gap is at
generations 6–7. That is precisely where the archival work should go, and it is
item 1 in [open-questions.md](open-questions.md).

## Other Kilians on the plate

Named outside the numbered centre line, and all documented:

- **Lucas Kilian** (1579–1637), brother of Wolfgang — painter, draughtsman,
  engraver; trained in the Custos orbit, seasoned in Italy 1601–04.
- **Bartholomäus Kilian II** (1630–1696), son of Wolfgang.
- **Wolfgang Philipp Kilian** (1654–1732), son of Philipp.
- **Philipp Andreas Kilian** (1714–1759) — in the Deutsche Biographie.
- **Georg Christoph Kilian** (1709–1781) — engraver, collector, and the family's
  own chronicler. His *Kunst und Ehrengedächtnis des Kilian'schen Geschlechts* is
  in the Staats- und Stadtbibliothek Augsburg, and it is the **single most
  important unexamined document for this line**.
