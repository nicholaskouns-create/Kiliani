# Internal discrepancies

Places where the documents inside this archive contradict each other. Recorded
rather than reconciled, because picking a winner without a source would be
inventing the answer.

**Updated after the October 2026 research pass.** Four are now resolved, one is
reversed against this archive, and three new ones have appeared — including one
that outranks everything else here.

---

## ⚠⚠ 0. Two incompatible paternal lines — NEW, and the most serious

The *Documented Family Tree* plate gives an entirely different paternal
ancestry from every other plate: Johann Conrad Cuntze (1710) → … → Raymond Earl
Koontz (1900–1985) → **Donald Ray Kouns 1935–2011**, with **no Joseph Jost
Cuntze and no Germanna migration at all**. Every other plate gives Henchin
Cuntze (c. 1500) → … → Edgar Kouns (1902–1967) → **Donald Ray Kouns
1932–2003**.

Nothing overlaps but the surname and the endpoint. **Donald Ray Kouns has two
different sets of dates and two different fathers.**

**To settle:** Donald Ray Kouns's **death certificate**, which names his father.
One document eliminates one line.

Full treatment: [paternal-kouns-line.md](paternal-kouns-line.md).

## ✅ 1. Alexander Killion's wife — RESOLVED

**Elizabeth "Lizzie" Hill (17 Aug 1895 – 20 Dec 1973)**, married Alex Killion
8 June 1910.

The obituary was right. The plates confirm it with both dates, the marriage date,
her parents and her burial place.

**And the error's source is now visible:** *Edna Alice Reynolds* (1861–1956) was
Lizzie's mother's younger sister — a great-aunt. One plate grafts her given name
onto Lizzie's position in the chart.

Detail: [reynolds-hill-branch.md](reynolds-hill-branch.md).

## ✅ 3. Which Passio minor manuscript is earliest — RESOLVED

**Vienna, ÖNB, Cod. 552, s. IX med.**, per the Bavarian Academy repertory. St
Gallen 566 is s. ix ex./x in.; e-codices dates it "around 900". German
Wikipedia's c. 870 has no cited basis.

The reading text had declined to assert the Vienna date because it could not
check it. The answer was the date it set aside.

Detail: [../texts/passio-minor/witnesses.md](../texts/passio-minor/witnesses.md).

## ⚠ 6. Bartholomäus Kilian I's death year — REVERSED AGAINST THIS ARCHIVE

| Year | Source | Standing |
|---|---|---|
| **1587/88** | Neue Deutsche Biographie; Bosls Bayerische Biographie | **better founded** |
| 1588 | CERL Thesaurus; Web Gallery of Art | |
| 1583 | *Augsburger Stadtlexikon*; *ADB* (1882) | older |

This archive previously asserted that the plate's 1588 was wrong and 1583 was
authoritative. **That correction was itself wrong.** The NDB ties the death to
Dominicus Custos's 1588 marriage to the widow Maria Pfeyffelmann and gives

**The plate was closer to right than the correction applied to it.** Cite

## ✅ 8. BHL numbers — PARTLY RESOLVED

**BHL 4660** and **BHL 4661** are confirmed through the Bavarian Academy
repertory and the *Index scriptorum novus Mediae Latinitatis*.

**New:** the *Index scriptorum* cites "BHL 4660-4660a", which indicates a
**sub-variant 4660a** this archive had not recorded.

**BHL 4662–4664**, which the ecclesiastical review attaches to the *maior*,
remain **unverified**. The *BHL* itself was not consulted. Do not cite the wider
range.

## 9. "Standard critical collation" — resolved, and the error is in a kept file

The ecclesiastical review and the earlier plates call **Emmerich (1896)** the
standard critical collation. **Levison (1910)** is. Emmerich prints the texts.

Corrected throughout the archive; listed here because a reader will meet the
error in [scholarship/ecclesiastical-review.txt](../scholarship/ecclesiastical-review.txt),
which is kept as written.

## 10. Which name the living endpoint carries

The maternal plate terminates in **Nicholas "Nick" Kouns**, the paternal in
**Nicholas Shane Kouns**. The same person, named differently on the two charts.

Not an error. Kept because it is a small instance of what the archive is about:
one person held by two lines, under two versions of a name.

## ⚠ 11. Alex Killion's dates — NEW

Four plates, four readings: 1885–1961, **27 Sept 1848** – 5 Jan 1961,
1884–1961, 1884–1961.

The 1848 would make him **112**. It is almost certainly picked up from **Arthur
Killion (1848–1920)**, directly adjacent on the same plate. **1884** is the
better reading.

Same plate, same class of error: **George Thomas Hill as 17 Aug 1828 –
11 Jan 1943**, which would be 115 years. Three other plates give **1878**.

Detail: [reynolds-hill-branch.md](reynolds-hill-branch.md).

## ⚠ 13. Benedict XVI's Erfurt homily — NEW, and a correction to this archive

This archive's bibliography attributed the fuller sentence — "Even before the
Anglo-Saxon missionaries, Saint Kilian, an itinerant missionary from Ireland…" —
to the **official** vatican.va text in seven languages.

It belongs to the **provisional text released through Vatican Radio**. The
official text names Kilian among the Erfurt patrons but does not carry that
sentence.

Detail: [../cult/worldwide.md](../cult/worldwide.md).
