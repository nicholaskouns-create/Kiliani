# The Church History Biographical Database

Four entries carrying this family's surnames, from the Church's own register.

## What this source is

The Church History Biographical Database is maintained by the Church History
Department of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. It holds biographical
entries on over 100,000 early Latter-day Saints: more than 61,000 pioneers who
travelled to Salt Lake City between 1847 and 1868, 44,000 missionaries who served
between 1830 and 1940, Mormon Battalion participants, and Church leadership. It was
formed in 2022 by merging the Pioneer Database and the Missionary Database.

**It is records-based.** Church History Library researchers built it by examining
company rosters, trail diaries, autobiographies, newspaper articles, local Church
records and other then-contemporary sources. That makes it a different class of
source from a user-edited tree: the entries derive from rosters, and the database
says where its readings come from — including where a roster spells a name
differently from the profile.

Entries are addressed by a permanent `RID`.

## 1. Susan Coons, who married a Killion — 1852

**The find.** Both of this family's surnames appear in one marriage, on one roster,
a century before Anna Rose Killion married Donald Ray Kouns.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Susan Killian**, birth name **Susan Coons** | b. 1 Feb 1838, d. 15 Sept 1920 |
| **Jacob Killian** | b. 31 Dec 1828, d. 14 Aug 1901 |
| Company | **Joel Edmunds Company**, 10 June – 8 Sept 1852 |

The database records, for both of them, that **the surname on the roster is
"Killion"** — not Killian. The register is explicit that the contemporary document
and the profile heading disagree, and it preserves both.

**Why it matters here.** This archive already carries *Coons* as a form of the
paternal surname: Christian Kouns / Kunz / **Coontz** (1759–1837), and Balthasar
Koontz / Balser **Counts** (1735–1835). A Coons marrying a Killion, with *Killion*
the spelling on the 1852 roster, is the two lines of this archive meeting in the
Church's own record.

**Not yet a claim of kinship.** Nothing here links Susan Coons or Jacob Killian to
this family. The surnames, the spellings and the period are right; the connection is
unproven.

## 2. Eleanor Kouns — Mormon Battalion generation

**William Cook Prows**, b. 11 June 1827 in **Kanawha, Virginia**; father Thomas
Prows III, mother **Eleanor Kouns**. Appointed Private, **Mormon Battalion Company
B**, 16 July 1846 – 16 July 1847.

Kanawha sits in the Greenbrier country where this archive places **Christian Kouns
(1759–1837)**, who moved Shenandoah → Greenbrier. Right surname, right region, right
generation for a daughter of that family. Unproven.

## 3. John Killian, 1796–1858 — missionary, out of Lincoln County

`RID PER-9XS7DXJ`

| | |
|---|---|
| Born | 25 April 1796, **Lincoln County, North Carolina** |
| Died | 10 November 1858 |
| Mother | Mary Haskins |
| Residence when called | **Kirtland, Ohio** |
| Priesthood office | Elder |
| Served | Illinois — North America Mission, 28 January 1845 (age 48) |

The FamilySearch profile for the same man (`KWVQ-326`) gives his father as **Philip
Killian**, mother as Mary Hoskings, has him moving to Missouri after the War of 1812
and marrying Mrs. Lydia Hopper Conley in 1818 in Howard County, Missouri.

**Lincoln County, North Carolina is the German Kilian settlement** — the country of
**Andreas Killian (1702–1788)**, who came to America from Germany in 1732 and whose
name in Germany was *Kilian*. This archive's **William Killion (1768–1850)** is
placed in North Carolina in that same milieu before moving to Tennessee.

This is the most promising lead in the file: it puts a documented Killian family of
German origin in the exact county and decade where this archive's American maternal
line begins. **It does not yet connect to William.**

## 4. The Thomas Killian family — to Utah, 1851

| Person | Age in 1851 |
|---|---|
| **Thomas Killian** | 30 |
| **Miriam Elizabeth Demaris** *or* **Sampson** — see below | 27 |
| **James Laman Killian** | 4 |
| **Andrew W Killian** | 2 |
| **Lydia Ann Killian** | 0 |

**Andrew W Killian** — b. 12 Feb 1848, **Lafayette, Missouri**; d. 25 April 1916, Annis.
**Lydia Ann Killian** — `RID PER-VLHPC33`, b. 18 Oct 1850; d. 26 Nov 1933.

The database states: *evidence from genealogical and church ordinance records proves
the Killian family travelled to Utah in 1851*, and that **further research is needed
to determine the name of the company they travelled with** — an open question the
register marks on itself.

### A conflict inside the database

The mother is given as **Miriam Elizabeth Demaris** on Andrew's entry and **Miriam
Elizabeth Sampson** on Lydia Ann's. One woman, two surnames, in the same database,
in the same travelling group.

Recorded here on the same principle as every other conflict in this archive: noted,
not reconciled, because picking a winner without a source would be inventing the
answer.

## Standing

All four are **surname matches in a records-based Church register**. None is yet
tied to this family's line. They are filed because the database is built from
rosters and ordinance records rather than from user-submitted trees, and because
three of them land in the right surname, region and decade:

| Entry | Lands on |
|---|---|
| Susan Coons × Jacob Killion, 1852 | both of this archive's surnames, one marriage |
| Eleanor Kouns | Kanawha / Greenbrier, where Christian Kouns settled |
| John Killian, 1796 | Lincoln Co., N.C. — where William Killion's generation begins |
| Thomas Killian family, 1851 | Missouri → Utah; no overlap with this line yet |

**To settle any of them:** the Joel Edmunds Company roster for 1852, the Lincoln
County, North Carolina records for Philip Killian's household, and the Kanawha County
records for Eleanor Kouns's parentage.
