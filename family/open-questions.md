Ordered by how much depends on them. Drawn from the prioritised research plan in

## 0. ⚠⚠ Settle which paternal line is real — NEW, outranks everything

Two irreconcilable reconstructions of the Kouns ancestry circulate in this
archive, with different dates for Donald Ray Kouns himself.

**The discriminating record: Donald Ray Kouns's death certificate**, which names
his father. Kentucky death certificates are held by the Office of Vital
Statistics and indexed to 1911.

Secondary: his obituary; Social Security records; the 1940 census household.

One document eliminates one line. Nothing else in this directory is as cheap or
as decisive. See [paternal-kouns-line.md](paternal-kouns-line.md).

## 0b. Walk two Kentucky cemeteries

The burial notes on the plates name four graves in two cemeteries:

- **Lancaster Cemetery, Lancaster, Ky.** — Alex Killion; Elizabeth "Lizzie" Hill
  Killion
- **Wylie Graveyard, near Paint Lick, Madison County, Ky.** — George Thomas Hill;
  Leanna Reynolds Hill
- also named: **Salem Christian Church Cemetery, Poosey Ridge, Madison County**

With death certificates from Garrard and Madison counties, several rows in
[reynolds-hill-branch.md](reynolds-hill-branch.md) move from **likely** to
**documented** in an afternoon. This is the highest ratio of certainty to effort
anywhere in the family archive.

## 1. Resolve the Kilian origin problem ⭐ highest value

**Target repositories:** Stadtarchiv Augsburg; Staats- und Stadtbibliothek
Augsburg.

**The document to find:** Georg Christoph Kilian, *Kunst und Ehrengedächtnis des
Kilian'schen Geschlechts*, in the Staats- und Stadtbibliothek Augsburg. The
family's own chronicle, written by a family member who was also a collector and
engraver. **This is the single most important unexamined document for the
maternal line** — the family already wrote its own archive once, and nobody in
this project has read it.

**✅ The immediate aim is already answered.** "Hans Kilian (1516–1583)" is a
conflation: the printer's birth year welded to Bartholomäus I's *ADB* death year.
Bartholomäus came from Silesia, which rules out an Augsburg-born father. See
[maternal-killion-line.md](maternal-killion-line.md).

**The remaining aim is positive, not negative:** what genealogy does the family's
own chronicle actually record for its origins? And **read Albert Hämmerle, *Die
Augsburger Künstlerfamilie Kilian* (1922)**, which is probably the only study to
have used it.

**Search terms:** Kilian · Killian · Hans Kilian · Bartholomäus Kilian ·
Goldschmied · Bürgerbuch · Meisterbuch · Testament

## ✅ 2. "St Clegwallabog Killian" — DONE. It is fiction.

No such saint exists in the *BHL*, any martyrology, the Bollandist indexes or any
Augsburg reference. The string appears only in FamilySearch, Geni, Ancestry member
content and a **Wikidata snippet that merged the record of the seventh-century
saint into a placeholder person** — which is where the "Saint" came from.

**Disposition: remove from the lineage.** Full verdict in
[maternal-killion-line.md](maternal-killion-line.md).

## 3. Close the generation 6–7 gap

The break between the Augsburg record and the American record — Johannes Jacob
Balthasar Kilian (1689–1742) and Johann/Andreas Killion (1732–1781). Everything
downstream is provisional until this is bridged, however well attested it is in
itself.

Note the competing candidate: the goldsmith **Johannes Kilian, 1623–1697**, is
the Johannes most visible in museum records, and he is the wrong generation.

## 4. Push the pre-1714 Cuntze line into parish and tax records

**Target repositories:** church and regional archives for Oberfischbach /
Niederndorf / Siegen-Wittgenstein; surviving guild and tax material for the
steelsmiths' and toolmakers' guild.

**Record series to locate:** the 1566 tax list attributed to Henchin Cuntze;
marriage and baptism entries for the 1667 Oberfischbach generation; guild
admissions for Johannes / Jost and brothers.

**Search all variants:** Cuntze · Kuntze · Kuntz · Koontz · Coons · Counts ·
Kouns · Kunz · Coontz

⚠ **But do item 0 first.** If the *Documented Family Tree* line is the real one,
the Niederndorf research is the wrong family.

## 5. Tighten the Germanna-to-frontier chain

**Target repository:** Library of Virginia.

**Inspect the image sets for:** Spotsylvania Will Book A; Stafford County Will
Book 1729–48; land patents; Prince William and Fauquier records; Hebron Lutheran
and associated church materials.

**Aim:** move from "Joseph Jost is anchored" to "the next two generations are
anchored" — Georg Heinrich Koontz, Balthasar, Christian.

## 6. Rebuild the American Killion strand from county records

Not from derivative trees.

**Counties:** Claiborne Co., Tennessee; then Laurel, Garrard, Lincoln and Boyle
counties, Kentucky.

**Pull:** marriage books for John J. Killion; deed indexes for the 1849 move to
Kentucky; probate and cemetery records for Arthur Killion; the death certificate
or death register entry for Alex J. Killion — which also settles
[discrepancy 1](discrepancies.md).

**Search variants:** Kilian · Killian · Killion, and phonetic neighbours where
handwriting is poor.

The Irish end of this is now scoped separately in
[irish-leads.md](irish-leads.md), with three corrections to how the plate
transmits the leads.

## 7. Resolve George W. Kouns (1863–1939)

The most urgent unresolved paternal row; same-name confusion already visible.

Work outward from Edgar Kouns (1902–1967) and Donald Ray Kouns (1932–2003) using
death certificates, obituaries, census households, probate and cemetery records
in Greenup, Boyd, Lawrence and neighbouring counties. Once George W. is cleanly
identified, Christian → George → Edgar should firm up.

## What a completed pass would look like

Not a proven line to the seventh century — that is not available and the archive
should stop wanting it.

A completed pass would mean: the Augsburg chronicle read; the Clegwallabog
insertion settled either way; the 1714 migration anchored two generations deeper
on both sides of the Atlantic; the Kentucky counties worked; and every row in
both line files carrying either **documented** or a named record series still to
inspect.

That is achievable. It is also, unlike descent from a saint, worth having.
