# Plates

The family's own charts and the webpages they were built from, kept as issued.

These are **[E3] primary artefacts of the family's self-documentation**. They are
not corrected in place. Where a plate contains an error, the error is recorded in
[../discrepancies.md](../discrepancies.md) and the plate stays as printed —
the same principle that keeps the first *Passio* source plate intact in
[../../recensions/](../../recensions/).

| File | What it shows |

Both pages are from the amateur Killion/Killian genealogy site that is the
single source of all five [Irish leads](../irish-leads.md). Its author labels his
own speculation as speculation, which is more than many published sources manage.

## Known errors in the plates

Recorded here so a reader meets them at the door. Detail in
[../discrepancies.md](../discrepancies.md).

| Plate | Error |
|---|---|
| expanded | **Alex Killion as 27 Sept 1848** – 5 Jan 1961 → 112 years. Read 1884. The 1848 comes from Arthur Killion directly above. |
| expanded | **George Thomas Hill as 17 Aug 1828** – 11 Jan 1943 → 115 years. Three other plates give 1878. |
| two-column | **"Edna E. Hill"** in Elizabeth "Lizzie" Hill's position. Edna Alice Reynolds was Lizzie's great-aunt. |
| two-column | **Francena Baker as 1840–1899**; other plates give 1834–1899. |
| all maternal charts | **"St Clegwallabog Killian (1490–1555)"** — no such person. [Verdict](../maternal-killion-line.md). |
| all maternal charts | **"Hans Kilian (1516–1583)"** — two men fused. [Verdict](../maternal-killion-line.md). |
| *Documented Family Tree* | A paternal line irreconcilable with every other plate, including different dates for Donald Ray Kouns. |

None of these diminishes the plates. They are handsome, detailed, and they
preserve material — the burial notes, the 1952 reunion photograph caption, the
ten siblings with their spouses and child counts — that exists nowhere else in
this archive.
