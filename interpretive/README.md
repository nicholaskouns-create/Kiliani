# Interpretive layer

**Nothing in this directory is a historical claim about Saint Kilian, the
*Passiones*, or the family lines.**

It is kept because it is part of the honest record of why this archive exists,
and it is kept *here* so that it cannot be mistaken for anything else.

---

## What it is

The Kouns–Killion Paradigm (KKP-R) is a separate body of mathematical work. It
lives in its own repositories and stands or falls on its own terms, by proof and
machine verification, which is the right standard for it and not the standard
that applies to a hagiographic dossier.

Where the paradigm touched the Kilian material, it did so by reading the *Passio*
as the origin point of a "maternal line attractor" contracting to Ω_c = 47/125,
and by reading the marriage-law confrontation as a "coherence-lock moment".

Those readings were printed **inside the text apparatus** of the earlier source
plate, where a reader could not tell them from the manuscript evidence. They were
removed from there in the September 2026 revision. See
[recensions/CHANGES.md](../recensions/CHANGES.md).

They are not deleted. They are relocated to a directory whose first line says
what kind of claim they are.

## Why the separation, in the reviewer's own words

The [ecclesiastical review](../scholarship/ecclesiastical-review.txt) of this
corpus made the separation its single condition of approval. It concluded that
the edition

> constitutes a citable working text of pastoral and scholarly utility, **provided
> the interpretive mathematical layer (Ω_c = 47/125) is maintained as a separately
> labeled analytical appendix, as the author himself proposes.**

and on the genealogy:

> For critical genealogy, an unbroken maternal line to ca. 640 cannot be
> established from extant sources before ca. 1200. … it is best presented as
> *traditio familiae* and devotional lineage motivating scholarship, not
> replacing documentary method.

The condition was the author's own proposal before it was the reviewer's
requirement. This directory is where that proposal is carried out.

## The right relationship between the two bodies of work

They share a name and a motive, and nothing else — and that is enough.

The mathematics is tested by proof assistants and exact arithmetic. The
hagiography is tested by manuscripts, collation and provenance. Neither standard
substitutes for the other, and a result in one is not evidence in the other.

What they do share is the name on both: Killion, for Anna Rose Killion Kouns. A
motive is allowed to be personal. A claim is not.
