Witnesses

The seven manuscripts Emmerich collated

Shelfmarks, dates and foliation exactly as he gives them in his manuscript list. Where the holding library serves a IIIF manifest, the folios open here and the images are streamed from that library — they are not copied into this repository.

The witness list records nineteen manuscripts from the repertory. These seven are the ones Emmerich actually used.

Images served by the holding libraries under their own terms. St Gallen 566 is published by e-codices under CC BY-NC. Nothing here is redistributed; each viewer streams from the library's own IIIF endpoint.

Five manifests are not yet located. They are marked as such rather than guessed, and tracked in the verification queue.
