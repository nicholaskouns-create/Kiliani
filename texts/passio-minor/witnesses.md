# Passio minor — manuscript witnesses

**The earliest-witness question is now settled.** On the best repertory evidence
it is **Vienna, ÖNB, Cod. 552, s. IX med.** — earlier than St Gallen 566.

---

## How that was resolved

| Source | Claim | Standing |
|---|---|---|
| ***Geschichtsquellen des deutschen Mittelalters***, werk/5445 (rev. 9 Apr 2026) | Wien, ÖNB, Cod. 552, f. 107v–112r, **s. ix med.** | **[A] — the Bavarian Academy repertory; follow this** |
| [Research report](../../scholarship/research-report.md) | Vienna 552 is mid-ninth century and earlier than St Gallen | **confirmed** |
| [Reading text, Sept 2026](reading-text-la-en.md) | St Gallen 566 (c. 870) is "the oldest known manuscript"; the Vienna dating is unverified | **superseded** |
| German Wikipedia | St Gallen 566, c. 870, is oldest | **no cited basis** |
| e-codices | St Gallen 566 "around 900" / s. ix–x | **[A]** — consistent with the repertory, not with c. 870 |

The reading text's caution was the right instinct — it declined to assert the
Vienna date it could not check — and the answer turns out to be the one it set
aside. Cite Vienna 552 as earliest, and confirm its date in the ÖNB catalogue
when possible.

### Count discrepancies

The IRHT *Légendiers latins* database counts **10** manuscripts. *Geschichtsquellen*
notes that two of IRHT's Brussels entries (8495–8505, 8942) **appear to be
wrong**. The repertory's 19 is the fuller and better-checked list.

## Hannover Ms I 189 — the illustrated witness

More interesting than a shelfmark line allows. **[A]**

- A **Fulda** manuscript of the **last quarter of the tenth century**
- It carries the **earliest surviving picture cycle of the legend**
- Published in facsimile by **Cynthia Hahn**, *Passio Kiliani, Ps. Theotimus,
  Passio Margaretae, Orationes*, Codices selecti 83/1 (Graz, 1988), with a
  commentary volume

This is the route to seeing how the legend was imagined within two centuries of
the *Passio minor*'s composition, without travelling to Hannover.
