# Passio minor — manuscript witnesses

**An unresolved disagreement sits at the head of this list and is recorded rather
than hidden.** The sources inside this archive do not agree on which manuscript
is earliest.

| Source in this archive | Claim |
|---|---|
| [Reading text, Sept 2026](reading-text-la-en.md) | St Gallen 566, c. 870, is **the oldest known manuscript**. The Vienna "s. IX med." dating is **unverified** and was therefore excluded. |
| [Research report](../../scholarship/research-report.md) | **Vienna ÖNB 552 is mid-ninth century** and therefore earlier than St Gallen 566, which it dates s. IX ex. / X in. |
| [Earlier source plate](../../recensions/01-source-plate.md) | Lists Vienna 552 as s. IX med. without stating which is earliest. |

Until a repository catalogue entry is inspected directly, **this archive takes no
position**. The question is open and is item 2 in the
[verification queue](verification-queue.md).

## Principal witnesses

| Siglum | Repository and shelfmark | Date | Folios / pages | Status here |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **V** | Vienna, Österreichische Nationalbibliothek, Cod. 552 | s. IX med. *(contested)* | fols 107v–112r | date unverified |
| **G** | St Gallen, Stiftsbibliothek, Cod. Sang. 566 | c. 870 / s. IX ex.–X in. | pp. 212–219 | verified against the German Wikipedia *Passio Kiliani* article only |
| **M** | Munich, Bayerische Staatsbibliothek, Clm 4585 | s. X *(plate gives s. IX–XI)* | fols 115r–119r | shelfmark and date unverified |
| **H** | Hannover, Ms I 189 | s. X | fols 1r–11r | illustrated witness; not inspected |
| **W** | Würzburg, M.p.th.q. 64 | — | fols 304r–306v | not inspected |

Later copies are recorded in Admont, Bern, Einsiedeln, Trier, Wolfenbüttel and
other collections. None has been inspected for this archive.

## Facsimile

Cynthia J. Hahn, *Passio Kiliani*, Codices selecti 83 (Graz, 1988), with
commentary volume. This is the route to the illustrated Hannover witness without
travel.

## Name variants across the tradition

Killena · Kyllena · Chilianus · Kilianus · Kylianus

Colonatus · Colmanus

Companion lists fluctuate; some *minor* witnesses carry additional figures.

## Desiderata for publication grade

Taken from the [ecclesiastical review](../../scholarship/ecclesiastical-review.txt),
which set these out as the conditions for calling this a critical edition:

- [ ] siglum table with **foliation verified at the repository**
- [ ] IIIF-compatible image citations
- [ ] explicit distinction between **Emmerich's emendations and manuscript
      readings** — at present the two are not separated anywhere in this archive
- [ ] line numbering
- [ ] full collation table of Emmerich variants
- [ ] image rights secured for St Gallen, Vienna and Munich plates

The conceptual stemma used in the earlier monograph must be replaced by a
witness-by-witness codicological collation before any final edition.
