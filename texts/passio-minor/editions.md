# Passio minor — printed editions and translations

**Cite the *Passio minor* from Levison.** Nothing in this repository replaces it.

---

## Standard critical edition

Wilhelm Levison, ed., *Passio Kiliani martyris Wirziburgensis*, in *Monumenta
Germaniae Historica, Scriptores rerum Merovingicarum* 5 (Hannover, 1910).

**Page range unresolved.** Two sources in this archive disagree:

| Source | Pages given |
|---|---|
| [Reading text](reading-text-la-en.md) | 711–728 |
| [Research report](../../scholarship/research-report.md) | 722–728 |

The wider range probably includes Levison's introduction and the *maior*; the
narrower probably covers the *minor*'s text alone. **Verify before citing.**
Item 5 in the [verification queue](verification-queue.md).

## Emmerich, 1896 — and a correction

Franz Emmerich, *Der heilige Kilian, Regionarbischof und Martyrer,
historisch-kritisch dargestellt* (Würzburg, 1896).

This archive's earlier plates cite **pp. 3–10** for the *minor*, and that page
range propagated into the monograph and the ecclesiastical review. The reading
text records it as **unconfirmed**. What *is* confirmed is that the *Passio
maior* is printed at **pp. 11–25**, which makes pp. 3–10 plausible for the
*minor* but not established.

Two further corrections belong here:

- Emmerich's 1896 book was a **historical-critical monograph, not a
  dissertation**. He received an honorary theological doctorate in the same year;
  he did not earn a doctorate through the Kilian study.
- Emmerich is described in this archive's earlier material as "the standard
  critical collation". He is not. **Levison is.** Emmerich is a nineteenth-century
  historical-critical monograph that prints the texts.

## Note on what this archive's text is

The Latin in [reading-text-la-en.md](reading-text-la-en.md) descends from the
earlier source plate, which described itself as "reconstructed from Emmerich and
earliest MSS". It is therefore at best a **reading text derived from a
nineteenth-century printing**, with four known defects marked in the
[apparatus](apparatus.md). It is not a transcription of any manuscript and not a
reproduction of Levison.
