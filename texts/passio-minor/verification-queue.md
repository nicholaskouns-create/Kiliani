# Passio minor — verification queue

Everything in this archive that is asserted but not yet checked against a source.
Nothing here is a flaw to be embarrassed about; an archive that cannot list its
own unchecked claims is the one with a problem.

Items are ordered by how much depends on them.

### 1. Collate the Latin against Levison ⚠ blocks everything

*MGH Scriptores rerum Merovingicarum* 5 (Hannover, 1910).

Four specific defects, in priority order:

- [ ] **n. 8** — restore the full royal title behind *Pippini regis*. This is the
      evidence for Levison's c. 840 dating and the archive has abridged it away.
- [ ] **n. 4** — supply the missing sentence in which Kilian resolves to go to
      Rome. Without it the text implies they sought the pope at Würzburg.
- [ ] **n. 6** — resolve *Cui dux promittens*, which has no finite verb.
- [ ] **n. 7** — confirm the conjunction supplied as ⟨dum⟩.

⚠ **And the closing chapter, which is worse than an abridgement.** Partly
recovered: c. 15 begins

> *Inlustrante autem Domino merita eorum, cum consilio et praecepto Zachariae
> papae **mediante Bonifatio archiepiscopo**, a Burchardo **primo
> Wirziburegensium episcopo** de tumulo honorifice sublevati sunt…*

**This archive's closing sentence is a different sentence**, missing Boniface,
missing "first bishop", and reducing *Pippino primo orientalium Francorum rege*
to a bare *Pippini regis*.

- [ ] Recover the **full** closing sentence and its *Amen* doxology from p. 728.
      Only the fragment above is in hand, and at second hand.
