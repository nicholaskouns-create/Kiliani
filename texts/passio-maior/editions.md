# Passio maior — printed editions and translations

**There is no universally adopted modern critical edition of the *Passio
maior*.** It still lacks one based on a complete stemmatic reassessment of all
known witnesses. This is a gap in the field, and it is the single clearest
opening for original work in the whole Kilian dossier.

| Editor | Work | Year | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Laurentius Surius | — | 1573 | **Incomplete** |
| Serarius | — | 1598 | Republication of Surius |

German translation: **Bigelmair**.

Compare the *minor*, which has had a modern critical edition since Levison in
1910. See [../passio-minor/editions.md](../passio-minor/editions.md).

## The opportunity

A critical edition of the *maior* would need:

- collation of the ~40 witnesses, beginning with Dublin NLI 19375 (possibly the
  author's autograph), Karlsruhe Aug. perg. 84, Munich Clm 18955 and Vatican
  Vat. lat. 8565
- a stemma built from that collation rather than assumed
- the relationship to the *minor* established line by line, which is also what
  would settle the attribution to Stephen of Novara on textual rather than
  circumstantial grounds

This is a defined, bounded, genuinely unfilled piece of scholarship.
