## On the Vatican manuscript

Vat. lat. 8565 is a **genuine Vatican Library witness to the medieval *Passio
maior***. It is worth being exact about what that does and does not mean, because
this archive's earlier material was not.

"Vatican document" covers two quite different things:

1. **Manuscripts now held by the Vatican Library.** Vat. lat. 8565 is one.
2. **Official documents issued or published by the Holy See** — bulls, curial
   acts, register entries.

Vat. lat. 8565 belongs to the first category only. It is **not** a papal decree,
**not** an act of canonisation, and **not** a document of the Roman curia. Where
a manuscript is kept today does not convert its text into an official papal act.

## The Dublin manuscript

If the attribution to Stephen holds, NLI 19375 may be the author's own copy. That
makes it the single most consequential witness in the dossier and the obvious
first target for any serious codicological pass — and it is in Dublin, which is
a pleasing symmetry for a text about an Irishman at Würzburg.
