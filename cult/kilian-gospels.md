# The Kilian Gospels

**Würzburg, Universitätsbibliothek, M.p.th.q.1a** — the *Kilians-Evangeliar*

The book traditionally associated with the saint, and the material object that
connects the Würzburg cult to a surviving artefact of roughly the right century.

## Location history

Kept in **Würzburg Cathedral until 1803**, and in the **University Library** ever
since. The 1803 date is the secularisation that also ended the Kiliani feast —
see [relics-and-pilgrimage.md](relics-and-pilgrimage.md).

The archive's earlier material described it as "his reputed New Testament
preserved until 1803 in the cathedral treasury", which is correct as far as it
goes. It is a Gospel book, not a New Testament, and it is now locatable by
shelfmark and readable online.

## What it is and is not

A **late sixth- or early seventh-century Gospel book**, which makes it roughly
contemporary with the saint's lifetime as the tradition dates it — and therefore
a genuinely remarkable survival.

It is **not** demonstrably Kilian's own book. The association is traditional. The
codex is older than the *Passio minor* that first tells his story by something
like a century and a half, which is the right way round for a relic but is not
evidence of ownership.

## Related
