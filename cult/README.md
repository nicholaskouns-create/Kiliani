## The one-line version

The cult is **pre-congregational**: it rests on an episcopal elevation of the
relics, not on any papal act, because formal papal canonisation did not yet
exist. Its continuity is a matter of bones that were lost in 1795, rediscovered
in 1849, moved for safety in the Second World War, and returned in 1949 — and of
a pilgrimage week whose present form dates only from 1935.

That is a more interesting history than unbroken continuity would have been, and
it is the actual one.
