## A caution about two of these

**The ecclesiastical review** calls Emmerich (1896) "the standard critical
collation". He is not; **Levison (1910)** is. It also repeats "pp. 3–10" for
Emmerich's *minor*, which is unconfirmed, and dates the *minor* to c. 730–752,
which is narrower than the evidence supports. The review is kept as written — it
is a genuine assessment and its conditions for publication grade are the most
useful list in the archive — but it is not an authority on the dating or the
editorial history. See [family/discrepancies.md](../family/discrepancies.md).

**The modern-archive monograph** reproduces the uncorrected translations
("Scottish land", "southern region", "Gallonus", "Arnualdus") and prints the
maternal line with St Kilian as generation 1. Both are superseded. See
[recensions/CHANGES.md](../recensions/CHANGES.md) and
[family/README.md](../family/README.md).

Neither is edited here. Correcting a document in place would destroy the evidence
of what it said, which is the same principle that keeps the first source plate
intact in [recensions/](../recensions/).
