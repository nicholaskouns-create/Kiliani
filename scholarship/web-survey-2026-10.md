# Web survey, October 2026

**"Saint Kilian and the Kilian, Killian and Killion Name: An Evidence-Graded
Survey"** — a commissioned research sweep of published scholarship, repertories,
library catalogues, museum records and genealogical sources, graded by evidence
class.

Its findings are distributed through this archive rather than left in one block.
This file records where each finding landed, so nothing is orphaned.

**Evidence classes used by the survey**, retained throughout the archive:

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[A]** | Primary record, or an authoritative library, museum or repertory reference |
| **[B]** | Peer-reviewed or academic scholarship |
| **[C]** | Derivative genealogical compilations — FamilySearch, Geni, WikiTree, Ancestry trees, personal pages |
| **[D]** | Tradition that has not been corroborated |

## What it added

- The **full 19-manuscript witness list** for the *minor*, with foliation and
  dates, replacing a five-item list with two unverified entries
- **33 shelfmarks** for the *maior*, against Levison's count of c. 40 and IRHT's 28
- **Kilian of Aubigny** and **Cillíne Droichtech of Iona** — two further saints
  of the name, with the conflation risk stated
- The **Kilian Gospels** located by shelfmark (Würzburg UB M.p.th.q.1a), dated by
  the library itself to northern France c. 600, and digitised
- The relic history: busts **melted for coin in 1794**, relics put away **1795**,
  **rediscovered 1849**, returned from wartime safekeeping **1949**
- That formal papal canonisation **begins with Ulrich of Augsburg in 993**, which
  is why no Kilian canonisation act exists or could
- **Griffith's Valuation** household counts and county distribution for Killian
  and Killion
- Corrections to three of the five Irish leads: **Barnacullen** not Barracullen,
  **Killian** not Kilian, **1 June** not 6 January
- A dozen post-2015 scholarly citations, including **Ruf (2020)** and
  **Klein (2022)**

## What it could not do

Recorded because an incomplete section is not a negative finding.

- **The *BHL* itself was not consulted.** BHL numbers are confirmed at one remove.
- **The full closing sentence of the *minor*** — only a fragment of c. 15 was
  recovered, and at second hand. **Read p. 728 in dMGH.**
- **Museum accession numbers** for the Augsburg engravings were not retrieved in
  the pass, and the survey declined to invent them. The numbers already in
  [family/augsburg-artistic-corpus.md](../family/augsburg-artistic-corpus.md)
  come from the earlier corpus and are unverified.
- **Modern surname distribution** outside Ireland
- **Austrian, British and Australian** Kilian dedications
- **United States county-level** Killion concentrations
- **BHL 4662–4664**
- The **shelfmark of Georg Christoph Kilian's family chronicle**

## Its five recommendations

Carried into the archive's own queues:

1. **Settle the *Passio minor*'s closing wording** by reading p. 728 of Levison
   in dMGH. Cite 722–728 for the text, 711–728 for the section. →
   [verification-queue](../texts/passio-minor/verification-queue.md) item 1
2. **Cite Vienna ÖNB Cod. 552 as the earliest listed witness**, following the
   Bavarian Academy, then confirm its date in the ÖNB catalogue. → done, and the
   confirmation step is queued
3. **Remove "St Clegwallabog" and the "Hans 1516" generation** from any lineage.
   Begin the Augsburg line with Bartholomäus I, d. 1587/88. → done
4. **Write to the Staats- und Stadtbibliothek Augsburg** for the shelfmark of
   Georg Christoph Kilian's chronicle, and read **Hämmerle (1922)**. →
   [family/open-questions.md](../family/open-questions.md) item 1
5. **Check every Irish lead** against irishgenealogy.ie, Griffith's and the
   Roscommon Poor Law Union records before entering it as fact. →
   [family/irish-leads.md](../family/irish-leads.md)

## One finding that concerns this archive's own publications

The survey flagged two pages on aims.healthcare:

- the **"Passio minor Kiliani — Reading Text with Translation"** (24 Sept 2026),
  whose closing sentence does not match the genuine c. 15 and which drops the
  royal title. The survey's instruction is **"do not use"** it as a text of the
  *minor*.
- the essay **"The Kentucky Killions: From Venerated Saint to Appalachia Poor"**,
  which repeats "Clegwallabog Killian (1490–1555)" and **asserts a descent from
  St Kilian that no source supports**.

On the first: the reading text says of itself that it is not a critical edition
and has not been collated against Levison. A reader took it at its word, checked
it, and found the defect where the page said to look. **That is the disclaimer
working.** What follows is that the collation is now urgent and the page should
carry the correction.

On the second: the essay's central genealogical claim is the one this archive
withdraws in [family/README.md](../family/README.md). The published page and the
archive now disagree, and the page is the older of the two.

Both are matters for the author to decide. They are recorded here because an
archive that tracks other people's errors and not its own publications is not
keeping an honest ledger.
