# Changes from the source plate to the reading text

Source plate → reading text, 24 September 2026.

Kept in full because corrections are only legible against what they corrected,
and because several of them will need to be made again by anyone who works from
the earlier plates circulating in the private corpus.

## Translation corrections

### *Scottica tellus* — the consequential one

| Was | Now |
|---|---|
| "whom the **Scottish land** brought forth" | "whom **the land of the Scots (Ireland)** brought forth" |

In early medieval Latin *Scotia* and *Scotti* mean Ireland. This error also ran
through the conflated *maior*'s conclusion ("The light that began in Scotland"),
corrected there too.

### *in Australium partem*

| Was | Now |
|---|---|
| "came to the **southern region** to the fortress which is called Würzburg" | "came into **Austrasia**, to the fortress called Würzburg" |

*Austria* / *Australia* in Frankish sources is **Austrasia**, the eastern
Frankish kingdom — not the adjective *australis*, "southern". Würzburg is in
eastern Francia.

### The companions

| Was | Now |
|---|---|
| "**Gallonus** and **Arnualdus** the priests" | "**Gallo** and **Arnual**" |

The plate's *Gallone* and *Arnuvale* are **ablatives**, governed by the ablative
absolute *congregatis … consociis*. The earlier English inflected the ablative as
though it were the nominative and then Latinised it back. The nominatives are
Gallo and Arnual.

### Smaller renderings

| Was | Now |
|---|---|
| "from there he **learned to rule the pontifical height**" | "he came in time to **govern as a bishop**" |
| "**corrupted by her allurements**" | "corrupted by **her own** enticements" |
| "**sacred letters**" | "**the sacred writings**" |

The first was mechanically literal; *culmen* is office, not altitude.

## Defects marked rather than repaired

The plate printed a continuous, fluent Latin text. It is not continuous, and four
places are defective. The revision marks each and **supplies nothing from
memory**.

| Note | Problem | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **n. 4** | A **lacuna** before the Roman journey. The sentence in which Kilian resolves to seek papal authorisation is missing, so the plate reads as though they sought the pope at Würzburg. | […] inserted; sentence **not reconstructed** |
| **n. 6** | *Cui dux promittens* has **no finite verb**. | ⟨…⟩ inserted; gap **not filled** |
| **n. 7** | *et, dux in venatione abesset* lacks a conjunction. | ⟨dum⟩ **supplied** and marked |
| **n. 8** | *Pippini regis* is a bare phrase. If it abridges a fuller royal title, **the abridgement removed the evidence for Levison's dating**. | flagged for restoration from Levison |

The conflated *Passio maior* in this archive silently repairs n. 6, printing
*promisit*. That is why the *minor* must be collated against Levison and not
against the *maior* text held here.

## Claims withdrawn

The plate carried a genealogical and mathematical apparatus inside the text
block. All of it is removed from the text and its apparatus.

Removed:

> "**Maternal Line Attractor Node 1: St. Kilian (ca. 640–689) → Nicholas Shane
> Kouns (1965–)**"

> "**Recursive contraction to Ω_c = 47/125**"

> "The text is the **earliest written trace of the maternal Kilian identity
> attractor**; the marriage-law confrontation constitutes the **coherence-lock
> moment** that initiates the long-range recursive contraction documented in the
> Kouns Family Tree."

> "Ready for insertion in KKP-R monographs, proof plates, or Nick Eigenstate
> Equations • Ω_c = 47/125"

The reason is given in the research report and is not a matter of taste: the
*Passio* records a celibate cleric among clerical companions and supplies no
spouse, no child and no hereditary descent. A fourteen-century chain cannot rest
on a modern plate that cites itself.

Where the material now lives: the lineage as a graded genealogical record in
[family/](../family/), and the mathematical reading in
[interpretive/](../interpretive/), labelled as interpretation. Neither is
deleted. Both are relocated to a directory that states what kind of claim it is
making.
