Fuit vir vitae venerabilis Killena nomine, quem Scottica tellus de magno edidit genere. Qui etiam a puerili aetate magnum habuit studium sacras discere litteras et in eis tam perfecte proficiens, ut exinde pontificale didicit regere culmen. Et tunc iam alio vocabatur nomine Chilianus, habens gratiam ad clerum et ad omnem populum. Qui etiam in tantum amore christianae religionis impletus, ut quicquid ad animarum lucrum, quicquid ad propagationem fidei pertinere cognoscebat, devota mente et pio operari studio non cessabat. | There was a man of venerable life named Killena, whom the Scottish land brought forth from a great family. From boyhood he also had great zeal to learn the sacred letters and advanced so perfectly in them that from there he learned to rule the pontifical height. And then he was already called by another name, Chilianus, having grace toward the clergy and all the people. He was also so filled with love of the Christian religion that whatever he knew pertained to the gain of souls or the propagation of the faith, he pursued with devoted mind and pious effort without ceasing.
Contigit autem, ut quadam die evangelica simul et voce dominica ammonitus esset, ubi dicitur (Luc. IX, 23): "Qui vult venire post me, abneget semet ipsum et tollat crucem suam et sequatur me." Haec secum beatissimus vir toto cordis ac mentis affectu pertractans, congregatis in unum suis consociis atque discipulis, id est Colonato ac Gallone et Arnuvale presbyteris et Totnano diacono, aliis septem illis adiunctis, coepit eos persuadere: propria contemni patriamque et parentes secundum Domini evangelium relinquere et nudos sequi Christum. Illi namque statim sacrae admonitioni beati viri obtemperantes firmiter coadunati, relictis omnibus, de propria perrexerunt patria et venerunt in Australium partem ad castellum, quod nominatur Wirziburg. | It happened, however, that on a certain day he was admonished by the evangelical and dominical voice where it says (Luke 9:23): "Whoever wishes to come after me, let him deny himself and take up his cross and follow me." The most blessed man, pondering these things with his whole heart and mind's affection, having gathered together his companions and disciples, that is, Colonatus and Gallonus and Arnualdus the priests and Totnan the deacon, with seven others joined to them, began to persuade them: to despise their own possessions and, according to the Lord's Gospel, to leave homeland and parents and follow Christ naked. They indeed, immediately obeying the sacred admonition of the blessed man and firmly united, having left all things, set out from their own homeland and came to the southern region to the fortress which is called Würzburg.
Sed illic venientes et apostolicum virum Johannem non invenientes, quoniam iam defunctus est, a sancto papa Conone praedictus Dei pontifex Killena amabiliter et honorifice susceptus est et, quod ad praedicandum verbum Dei petierat, cum omni benignitate consecutus est. | But coming there and not finding the apostolic man John, since he was already dead, the aforementioned pontiff of God, Killena, was received amiably and honorably by the holy Pope Conon and obtained with all benevolence what he had sought for preaching the word of God.
Revertens igitur cum suis sociis ad praedictum castellum Wirziburg, coepit ibi verbum Dei fiducialiter praedicare. Audiens autem dux Gozbertus verbum praedicationis eius, cum omni populo suo credidit et baptizatus est. | Returning therefore with his companions to the aforementioned fortress of Würzburg, he began to preach the word of God there confidently. Hearing the word of his preaching, Duke Gozbert believed with all his people and was baptized.
Non multo post tempore persuadebat illum devotus Domini pontifex Kilianus ut dimitteret uxorem suam Geilanam, quae fuerat uxor fratris eius, quia hoc christianae religioni contrarium esse cognoscebat. Cui dux promittens se facturum quod ei praeciperet. | Not long after, the devoted pontiff of the Lord, Kilian, persuaded him to dismiss his wife Geilana, who had been the wife of his brother, because he knew this to be contrary to the Christian religion. To whom the duke promised to do what he commanded him.
Geilana autem, corrupta suis illecebris, cum audisset haec, irata est valde et, dux in venatione abesset, misit servos suos qui beatum Kilianum cum sociis suis Colonato presbytero et Totnano diacono in stabulo equorum occiderent et corpora eorum ibi sepelirent cum vasis sacris et vestimentis et scripturis sanctis. | But Geilana, corrupted by her allurements, when she heard these things, was greatly angered and, while the duke was absent on a hunt, sent her servants who killed the blessed Kilian with his companions, the priest Colonatus and the deacon Totnan, in the horse stable and buried their bodies there with the sacred vessels and vestments and holy writings.
Postea vero, cum Burchardus episcopus Wirziburgensis, praecepto et consilio Zachariae papae et Pippini regis, corpora sanctorum martyrum cum honore transtulisset et in ecclesia beatae Mariae in monte collocasset, miraculis et sanitatibus claruerunt. | Afterward, however, when Bishop Burchard of Würzburg, by the precept and counsel of Pope Zacharias and King Pippin, had translated the bodies of the holy martyrs with honor and placed them in the church of blessed Mary on the mountain, they became renowned through miracles and healings.```
