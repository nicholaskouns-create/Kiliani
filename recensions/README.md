# Recensions

The editorial history of this archive's own text of the *Passio minor*.

Textual criticism is the discipline of knowing which reading came from which
witness, when, and who changed it. This directory applies that discipline to the
archive itself. The plates went through recensions exactly as the medieval text
did, and those recensions are evidence — of care taken, errors found, and claims
withdrawn.

## Why the first plate is kept unaltered

Because a correction is only legible against what it corrected. Silently
replacing the plate would destroy the evidence that the work was done. The plate
is kept as issued — the mistranslations, the "ca. 752" in the title, the word
"Critical", the "Citable Edition" footer, and the genealogical framing all still
in place.

It is not the archive's text. It is the archive's first draft, and it is dated.

## The shape of the correction

Three kinds of change happened, and they are worth distinguishing.

**Philological.** *Scottica tellus* is Ireland, not Scotland. *In Australium
partem* is Austrasia, not the south. *Gallone* and *Arnuvale* are ablatives;
the nominatives are Gallo and Arnual. These are straightforward errors with
straightforward fixes.

**Editorial honesty.** "ca. 752" removed from the title because it is one
hypothesis among several. "Critical" removed because the text is not a critical
edition. The "Citable Edition" footer removed because the text is not citable —
Levison is. Defective Latin marked rather than silently repaired, and the
Levison collation listed as outstanding rather than implied as done.

**Withdrawal of claim.** The genealogical apparatus — "Maternal Line Attractor
Node 1", "the earliest written trace of the maternal Kilian identity attractor",
"the coherence-lock moment that initiates the long-range recursive contraction",
"Ω_c = 47/125" — removed from the text and its apparatus entirely.

The third kind is the hardest and the most to the archive's credit. The first two
are scholarship. The third is giving something up.
